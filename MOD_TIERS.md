# Trollcraft III — Per-Mod Tiers

Each progression mod split into its own **early game**, **mid game** and **end game** stages.

- These stages are **inside each mod**. A mod's "early game" isn't always early in the pack: Draconic Evolution's early
  stage only starts once you can afford fusion crafting. [PROGRESSION_MODS.md](PROGRESSION_MODS.md) shows which mods
  are early–mid (Tier 1) and which are end game (Tier 2), and [STARTING_MODS.md](STARTING_MODS.md) covers the
  pack-wide order.
- **Move on when** is the item or milestone that opens the next stage.
- **Needs from outside** lists what the mod needs from other mods, other dimensions or bosses.
- Every step comes from the verified progression in [CROSS_REFERENCE.md](CROSS_REFERENCE.md), which has the sources.
  Names are the in-game names.

## Tier 1 — Major mods

### Tinkers' Construct

*3.11.2 · In-game guide: Materials and You (given on first spawn)*

**Early game**
- Make Patterns, a **Part Builder** and a **Tinker Station**, then wood, stone and flint tools. Modifiers work from
  the very first tool.
- Craft **Grout**, smelt it into Seared Bricks, and build the **Seared Melter** with a Casting Table, Casting Basin
  and Faucet. Fuel it with solid fuel or lava.
- Cast molten copper onto Seared Bricks to make the **Smeltery Controller**, then build the smeltery with drains and
  tanks.
- **Move on when:** the smeltery is running.

**Mid game**
- Alloy Slimesteel, Rose Gold, Amethyst Bronze and Pig Iron. Use an alloy block to build the **Tinker's Anvil**,
  which unlocks broad tools. Thermal's alloy blocks can also work for the anvil.
- Explore slime islands: earthslime in the ocean, skyslime in the sky.
- In the Nether, mine **Nether Cobalt Ore** (densest at Y 8–32), make **Nether Grout**, and build the
  **Scorched Alloyer** and **Foundry**. The foundry gives byproducts but can't alloy.
- **Move on when:** you have **Blazing Blood** (melt blazes or blaze heads in the smeltery or foundry).

**End game**
- Use Blazing Blood as fuel to alloy **Manyullyn**, **Hepatizon**, **Queen's Slime** and Cinderslime. Lava isn't hot
  enough for these.
- Make netherite-tier tools and **Plate Armor**.
- Enderslime from End islands is mostly for the slimesuit.
- **Tinkers' Levelling Addon** gives tools extra slots as they level up.

**Needs from outside:** the Nether (cobalt, Nether Grout, blazes).

### Create

*6.0.8 + Crafts & Additions · In-game guide: Ponder (hold the Ponder key over an item)*

**Early game (andesite age)**
- Hand-craft **Andesite Alloy** from andesite and iron or zinc nuggets.
- Power: a **Water Wheel** or **Windmill**, then Shafts and Cogwheels. Make **Engineer's Goggles** to read stress.
- Build **Andesite Casing**, the **Mechanical Press**, the **Encased Fan** (washing and smelting) and Belts.
- Add the **Mechanical Mixer** and **Basin**. A mixer can also make Andesite Alloy in bulk.
- **Move on when:** you have caught a blaze for a **Blaze Burner**, which means a Nether trip.

**Mid game (copper and brass)**
- Copper: Copper Casing, pumps and pipes, the Spout and Item Drain, and **Steam Engines** on a boiler.
- Mix **Brass** with a Blaze Burner under the Basin, then make Brass Casing.
- Make **Rose Quartz**, then **Electron Tubes** and the **Deployer**.
- Make the **Precision Mechanism** by sequenced assembly. It unlocks the **Mechanical Arm** and
  **Mechanical Crafter**.
- Build **Crushing Wheels** for ore doubling.
- Crafts & Additions adds the **Rolling Mill** and the **Alternator** (rotation to FE power).
- **Move on when:** you can make the **Sturdy Sheet** (crushed obsidian filled with lava and pressed).

**End game (large-scale automation)**
- Make **Train Casing** for trains, stations and signals.
- Create 6.0 logistics: the **Packager**, **Stock Ticker**, **Package Frogport** and **Factory Gauge**.
- Crafts & Additions adds the **Electric Motor** (FE to rotation), **Tesla Coil** and **Accumulator**.
- Optional: Create Big Cannons.
- Create has no single final item. Its end game is scale.

**Needs from outside:** the Nether (a blaze for the Blaze Burner).

### Mekanism

*10.4.16 + Generators, Tools · In-game guide: none (use JEI, Just Enough Mekanism Multiblocks and the advancements)*

**Early game**
- Mine **Osmium**. Build a **Heat Generator**, which also makes passive power next to lava.
- Build the **Metallurgic Infuser** and make **Steel Ingots**, **Infused Alloy** and **Basic Control Circuits**.
- Make the **Steel Casing**, then the **Enrichment Chamber** for 2x ores. Raw ore gives about 1.33x; silk-touched
  ore blocks give the full 2x.
- **Move on when:** you have **Advanced Control Circuits** and oxygen from an **Electrolytic Separator**.

**Mid game**
- 3x: the **Purification Chamber** and Crusher.
- 4x: **Reinforced Alloy**, Elite circuits and the **Chemical Injection Chamber**, which uses Hydrogen Chloride.
- 5x: **Refined Obsidian** and **Atomic Alloy** for Ultimate circuits, then the Chemical Dissolution Chamber,
  Chemical Washer and Chemical Crystallizer. Silk-touched ore blocks give the full 5x.
- Mekanism Tools gear, and machine factories.
- **Move on when:** you have Fissile Fuel from the uranium chain (Yellow Cake Uranium, Isotopic Centrifuge).

**End game**
- Build the **Fission Reactor** and an **Industrial Turbine**.
- Turn its waste into **Polonium Pellets** and **Plutonium Pellets**, and make **HDPE Sheets**.
- Polonium unlocks the **MekaSuit** (each piece is built on the matching netherite armor piece), the **Meka-Tool**,
  **QIO** and the **Fusion Reactor**.
- The **SPS** makes antimatter pellets. These are only needed for the **Antiprotonic Nucleosynthesizer** and the top
  MekaSuit modules (Elytra, Gravitational Modulating, Teleportation).

**Needs from outside:** a full set of netherite armor for the MekaSuit.

### Immersive Engineering

*10.2.0 · In-game guide: Engineer's Manual (Book + Lever)*

**Early game**
- Craft the **Engineer's Manual** and **Engineer's Hammer**.
- Build a **Coke Oven** for Coal Coke and Creosote Oil. Creosote makes **Treated Wood**.
- Build the **Engineer's Workbench** for blueprints.
- First power: a **Windmill** on a **Kinetic Dynamo**, LV Wire Coils and an LV Accumulator.
- **Move on when:** you have **Blast Bricks**, which need Nether Brick and Magma Blocks from the Nether.

**Mid game**
- Smelt **Steel** in the Crude Blast Furnace.
- Make Light and Heavy Engineering Blocks.
- First multiblocks: the **Crusher** (ore doubling), **Metal Press** and **Improved Blast Furnace**.
- Find aluminum (bauxite) for the **Components** blueprint, which makes Vacuum Tubes and Circuit Backplanes.
- Optional power and tools: the Thermoelectric Generator, Mining Drill and Revolver.
- **Move on when:** you can make **Graphite Electrodes** (Squeezer for graphite dust, then the Metal Press).

**End game**
- **Arc Furnace**.
- Squeezer and Fermenter feed the **Refinery**, which makes Biodiesel for the **Diesel Generator**.
- **Excavator** for ore veins (find them with the Core Sample Drill).
- Railgun and Chemical Thrower.

**Needs from outside:** the Nether (Nether Brick and Magma Blocks for steel).

### Thermal Series

*Foundation 11.0.6 + Expansion, Dynamics, Innovation, Integration, Redstone Arsenal · In-game guide: Thermal
guidebook (Book + Crescent Hammer)*

**Early game**
- Mine tin, lead, silver, nickel and the other Thermal ores.
- Hand-craft **Bronze**, **Electrum**, **Invar** and **Constantan** with Fire Charges, then make gears.
- Make the **Redstone Flux Coil** and **Redstone Servo**, then a **Stirling Dynamo** for power.
- Build the **Machine Frame**, then the **Pulverizer**, **Redstone Furnace**, **Induction Smelter** and **Sawmill**.
- **Move on when:** you have a **Tinker's Workbench** to apply upgrades.

**Mid game**
- Storage and transport: the Redstone Flux Cell, **Fluxduct** and **Fluiduct**. Thermal has no item ducts in 1.20.
- Make **Signalum**, **Lumium** and **Enderium** (Enderium needs diamond dust).
- **Hardened** and then **Reinforced Integral Components** upgrade machines and dynamos.
- More machines: Magma Crucible, Phytogenic Insolator, Fluid Encapsulator, Multiservo Press, Sequential Fabricator.
- **Thermal Innovation** tools: Fluxbore, Fluxsaw, FluxoMagnet.
- **Redstone Arsenal** flux tools and armor (Flux-Infused Ingots can be hand-crafted).
- **Move on when:** you have Enderium, Lumium gears and Hardened Glass.

**End game**
- **Resonant Integral Components** (the top craftable upgrade; Resonant+ has no recipe).
- Fully upgraded machines and dynamos. Thermal's end game is efficiency, not a final item.

**Needs from outside:** nothing essential. Thermal's alloys also feed other mods: electrum for Modular Powersuits
batteries, and alloy blocks for the Tinker's Anvil.

### Ender IO

*6.2.18-beta · In-game guide: none (use JEI)*

**Early game**
- Light a fire on Overworld **bedrock** for **Grains of Infinity**.
- Build the **Primitive Alloy Smelter**, which runs on fuel. Alloy **Dark Steel**.
- Make the **Void Chassis** and **Basic Capacitor**. Every machine needs a capacitor installed.
- First machines: the **Stirling Generator**, **Alloy Smelter** and **SAG Mill**.
- **Move on when:** you have **Conductive Alloy** for Energy Conduits.

**Mid game**
- Energy, Item, Fluid and Redstone **Conduits**.
- Make **Energetic** and **Vibrant Alloy**, then the **Double Layer Capacitor** and Capacitor Banks.
- Photovoltaic Modules for solar power.
- In the Nether, get soul sand for **Soularium**. That gives the **Ensouled Chassis** and the **Slice'N'Splice**,
  which makes the **Z-Logic Controller**.
- **Move on when:** you have a Z-Logic Controller.

**End game**
- **Octadic Capacitor**.
- **Soul Binder**: soul crystals and the Soul Engine.
- **Powered Spawner**: mine a vanilla spawner for a Broken Spawner first.
- Enchanter, XP Obelisk, Travel Anchors and the Staff of Travelling.
- **End Steel** needs end stone from the End.

**Needs from outside:** the Nether (Soularium) and the End (End Steel).

### Applied Energistics 2

*15.4.11 + Advanced AE, AppliedE and other addons · In-game guide: Guide (Book in a Charger)*

**Early game**
- Build a **Charger** and a **Crank**. Charge a compass into a **Meteorite Compass**.
- Find a meteorite and mine the **Mysterious Cube** **without Silk Touch** for all four presses.
- Take the budding certus home.
- Make **Charged Certus Quartz**, then **Fluix Crystals** by throwing it into water with redstone and nether quartz.
- **Move on when:** you have an **Inscriber** and all three processors (Logic, Calculation, Engineering).

**Mid game**
- First network: an **ME Drive**, **Terminal**, **Energy Acceptor** and storage cells (start at 1k–4k).
- Add an **ME Controller** past 8 channels, and smart or dense cables.
- Growth Accelerators for steady certus.
- Autocrafting: the **Pattern Encoding Terminal**, **Pattern Provider**, **Molecular Assembler** and a crafting CPU.
- **Move on when:** you can make a **Singularity** in the Matter Condenser.

**End game**
- **Quantum Network Bridge**, **P2P Tunnels** and **Spatial IO**.
- **Advanced AE**: the Reaction Chamber, Quantum Alloy, the **Quantum Computer** multiblock and Quantum armor.
- **AppliedE** connects ProjectE's EMC to the network. It needs ProjectE Red Matter and a Transmutation Tablet.

**Needs from outside:** FE power from any tech mod. AppliedE needs ProjectE's end game.

### Botania

*1.20.1-456 · In-game guide: Lexica Botania (Book + any sapling)*

**Early game**
- Collect petals and build a **Petal Apothecary**.
- Make a **Pure Daisy** for **Livingwood** and **Livingrock**.
- Make the **Wand of the Forest**, an **Endoflame** (burns fuel for mana), a **Mana Spreader** (copper ingot) and a
  **Mana Pool**.
- Infuse **Manasteel**, **Mana Pearls** and **Mana Diamonds** in the pool.
- **Move on when:** you have a **Runic Altar**.

**Mid game**
- Make the elemental runes.
- Build the **Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate** and make **Terrasteel** (500,000 mana per ingot).
- Build the **Alfheim portal** (Elven Gateway Core and Natura Pylons, both made with Terrasteel). It's trade-only;
  players can't go through it.
- Trade for **Elementium**, **Pixie Dust** and **Dragonstone**.
- Better generating flowers and Botania tools.
- **Move on when:** you have **Gaia Pylons** (Elementium and Pixie Dust).

**End game**
- Place Gaia Pylons around a Beacon to fight the **Guardian of Gaia**, which drops **Gaia Spirit**.
- A **Gaia Spirit Ingot** summons the harder **Guardian II**, which drops the **Dice of Fate** (Relics of the Aesir).
- Gaia gear: the **Flügel Tiara** and the Gaia Mana Spreader.

**Needs from outside:** a Beacon, which needs a Nether Star from the Wither.

### Mystical Agriculture

*7.0.24 · In-game guide: Mystical Agriculture book (Book + Inferium Essence)*

**Early game**
- Mine **Inferium Ore** and **Prosperity Ore**, and pick up Inferium Essence from mobs.
- Grow **Inferium Seeds** on essence farmland.
- Make the **Infusion Crystal**, then upgrade essence on a crafting table: **Prudentium**, then **Tertium**.
- **Move on when:** you have an **Infusion Altar** with 8 Infusion Pedestals.

**Mid game**
- Make **resource seeds** on the altar (Prosperity Seed Base plus essence and the resource).
- Upgrade essence to **Imperium**.
- In the Nether, get **Soulium**. Fill **Soul Jars** (Soulium Dagger or Soul Extractor) for mob seeds.
- **Growth Accelerators**, essence tools and armor, the **Tinkering Table** and augments.
- Automation: the **Harvester** and **Seed Reprocessor**.
- **Move on when:** you have **Supremium Essence**.

**End game**
- **Master Infusion Crystal** (infinite uses).
- Elemental essences (Air, Earth, Water, Fire).
- **Mystical Enlightenment** at the Enchanter, then kill the Wither or Ender Dragon with that weapon for
  **Cognizant Dust**.
- Build the **Awakening Altar** with its pedestals and Essence Vessels for **Awakened Supremium** gear.
- Soulium Spawner.
- No Mystical Agradditions in the pack, so there's no Insanium tier and no cruxes.

**Needs from outside:** the Nether (Soulium), the Wither or Ender Dragon (Cognizant Dust).

### EvilCraft

*1.3.3 · In-game guide: Origins of Darkness (chest loot only)*

**Early game**
- Mine **Dark Ore** for **Dark Gems**.
- Find a Dark Temple and use its **Environmental Accumulator** with Weather Containers.
- Make **Dark Spikes** and a **Blood Extractor**, then collect blood from kills.
- **Move on when:** you have a **Dark Power Gem** (a Dark Gem thrown into 5+ blood source blocks).

**Mid game**
- Make **Hardened Blood Shards**, then the **Blood Infusion Core** and **Blood Infuser**.
- Upgrade the infuser with **Promises of Tenacity** (tiers 1–2).
- **Purifier**.
- Catch Vengeance Spirits in a **Box of Eternal Closure**.
- Build the **Spirit Furnace** multiblock (Dark Blood Bricks) and the **Spirit Reanimator**.
- **Move on when:** your Blood Infuser reaches **tier 3**.

**End game**
- **Piercing Vengeance Focus** gives **Vengeance Essence**, which infuses into **Garmonbozia**.
- Garmonbozia makes the **Sanguinary Environmental Accumulator** (it also needs an Environmental Accumulation Core
  from a temple) and the **Mace of Destruction**.
- Necromancer's Staff.

**Needs from outside:** nothing essential.

### Extreme Reactors 2

*2.0.97 · In-game guide: The Extreme Book (Yellorium Ingot over a Book)*

**Early game**
- Mine **Yellorite Ore** (low in the Overworld) for **Yellorium Ingots**.
- Smelt coal or charcoal into **Graphite Bars**.
- Build a **Basic reactor** (up to 5×5×5, passively cooled) with an Extreme Wrench.
- **Move on when:** the reactor has produced **Cyanite** (spent fuel).

**Mid game**
- Switch to **active cooling** and feed the steam to a **Basic Turbine**. Turbine parts need Cyanite.
- Build **Reinforced** reactors and turbines (needs steel).
- **Move on when:** you have enough Cyanite for a **Reprocessor**.

**End game**
- The **Reprocessor** turns Cyanite into **Blutonium**, a better fuel.
- Make **Ludicrite**, **Ridiculite**, **Inanite** and **Insanite** for the best turbine coils.
- **Fluidizer** and **Energizer**.

**Needs from outside:** steel (Mekanism or Immersive Engineering) for the Reinforced tier.

### Modular Powersuits

*2.3.13 + Numina · In-game guide: none (the Tinker Table shows module costs)*

**Early game** (once you have steel)
- Build the **Power Armor Tinker Table** and make Numina **Wiring**.
- Make the **Power Armor** pieces and the **Power Fist**.
- Install a **Basic Battery** (needs steel) so items can hold power.
- Make the **Solenoid**, **Servo Motor** and **Control Circuit**.
- First modules: Iron Plating, the Power Fist's pickaxe, axe and shovel, Sprint Assist and Jump Assist.
- **Move on when:** you have a **Computer Chip** and **Force Field Emitter**.

**Mid game**
- **Advanced** and **Elite Batteries**.
- Diamond Plating, Energy Shield, Night Vision and the Solar Generator.
- **Charging Base**.
- **Move on when:** you have an **Ion Thruster** and an **Ultimate Battery** (needs electrum).

**End game**
- Movement: **Jetpack**, **Flight Control**, **Blink Drive**.
- Weapons: **Plasma Cannon**, Railgun.
- **Netherite Plating**, Vein Miner and Tunnel Bore.

**Needs from outside:** steel (Mekanism or Immersive Engineering), electrum (Thermal), netherite.
Item names may show as raw text keys in-game (see PROGRESSION_MODS.md).

### Alex's Caves

*2.0.2 · In-game guide: Cave Compendium (Book + Cave Tablet + Gold Nugget)*

The order of the biomes is up to the player. This split is by how dangerous each content step is.

**Early game**
- Find an **Underground Cabin**: Cartographers sell maps to them, and mineshaft chests contain them.
- Collect **Cave Tablets** and translate them at the cabin's **Spelunkery Table**.
- Make **Cave Codices**, then craft a **Cave Biome Map**. Make the **Cave Compendium**.
- Visit whichever biome you get a map for first. Each biome's tablets come from different places, for example
  Magnetic Caves tablets from Bastion chests and Primordial Caves tablets from desert and ocean ruins.
- **Move on when:** you have a map to each biome you want to explore.

**Mid game**
- **Magnetic Caves:** the Magnetron drops the **Heart of Iron** for the **Magnetic Quarry**. The Teletor drops the
  **Telecore** for the **Galena Gauntlet**.
- **Toxic Caves:** uranium and sulfur. Get **Hazmat Armor**, then a Uranium Rod, the **Raygun** and the
  **Nuclear Furnace**.
- **Abyssal Chasm:** take a Submarine or Diving Suit. Trade with the Deep Ones for the **Magic Conch**,
  **Sea Staff** and **Ortholance**.
- **Forlorn Hollows:** the Forsaken drops Pure Darkness for the **Dreadbow**. The Watcher drops the Occult Gem for the
  Totem of Possession.
- **Candy Cavity:** the **Gum Worm** drops the Sweet Tooth for the **Sack of Sating**. Licowitches drop Radiant
  Essence for the **Conversion Crucible**.
- **Move on when:** you have Heavy Bones and an Amber Curiosity for the **Ominous Catalyst**.

**End game**
- Summon the **Luxtructosaurus**, the mod's main boss, for Tectonic Shards and the **Extinction Spear**.
- **Hullbreaker**: drops the Immortal Embryo.
- **Nuclear Bomb**.
- Make a **Tremorzilla Egg**, hatch it with a nuclear explosion and tame it.

**Needs from outside:** a Bastion, desert/ocean ruins, jungle temples, woodland mansions and witch huts for some Cave
Tablets.

### RFTools

*Base 5.0.7, Utility 6.0.7, Builder 6.0.10 (Tier 1) · Dimensions 11.0.11 (Tier 2) · In-game guide: Technology Guide
(Book + 3 Iron Nuggets)*

**Early game**
- Mine **Dimensional Shard Ore**.
- Make the **Machine Frame** and **Machine Base**, then **Crafter** Tier 1 and **Screens**.
- **Move on when:** you have steady FE power.

**Mid game**
- Teleporting: **Matter Transmitter**, **Matter Receiver** and **Dialing Device**.
- **Builder** with Shape Cards, then the **Shape Card (Quarry)**.
- **Machine Infuser**, Crafters Tier 2–3, Space Chamber.
- **Move on when:** you start finding **Lost Knowledge** (Endermen and chest loot).

**End game (RFTools Dimensions)**
- Build the **Researcher**, **Knowledge Holder** and **Dimlet Workbench**, then make dimlets. Absorbers capture
  specific blocks, fluids, biomes and structures.
- Use the **Enscriber** to turn an Empty Dimension Tab into a **Realized Dimension Tab**.
- Build the **Dimension Builder**. Keep it powered and chunkloaded.
- Carry a **Phased Field Generator** before entering an unpowered dimension.

**Needs from outside:** a lot of FE power for dimensions.

## Tier 1 — Smaller mods

### Hexerei

*0.4.2.3 · In-game guide: Book of Shadows (read it on an Altar)*

- **Early:** the Book of Shadows, an Altar, the **Mixing Cauldron**, the Herb Drying Rack and the Pestle and Mortar.
  Gather swamp herbs.
- **Mid:** the **Blood Sigil** makes **Bottles of Blood**. Then the **Willow Broom** (gold blocks), the
  **Witch Hazel Broom** (diamond), broom brushes and Witch armor.
- **End:** the **Mahogany Broom** (netherite), the **Broom Netherite Tip** (flies through lava) and the
  Crow Ankh Amulet (needs a Totem of Undying).

### Reliquary

*2.0.65 · In-game guide: none*

- **Early:** collect mob drops. They only drop on player kills, about 2–3% each, and Looting helps. Make the
  **Apothecary Mortar** and simple relics.
- **Mid:** the **Hunter's Handgun** with magazines, the **Apothecary Cauldron**, the **Tome of Alkahestry** (needs a
  Wither Skeleton Skull), the **Altar of Light** and the **Void Tear**.
- **End:** the **Infernal Tear**, **Phoenix Down**, mob charms and the **Charm Belt**. Pedestals automate relics.

### AgriCraft

*4.0.6 · In-game guide: Agricultural Journal (Book and Quill + any AgriCraft seed)*

- **Early:** Crop Sticks, the Magnifying Glass and **Seed Analyzer**. Cross-breed crops to raise their six stats.
- **Mid:** resource crops, each needing its ore block **under the soil**: **Ferranium** (iron), Cuprosia (copper),
  Lapender (lapis), Redstodendron (redstone), Aurigold (gold), Amathyllis (amethyst), Carbonation (coal), plus the
  modded-metal crops. Iron Crop Sticks.
- **End:** Nitor Wart (glowstone), Quartzanthemum, Emeryllis (emerald), **Diamahlia** (diamond) and **Nethereed**
  (netherite). Obsidian Crop Sticks, maxed 10/10 stats.

### Extra Utilities Reborn

*1.0.1 · In-game guide: none (some recipes are only in item tooltips)*

- **Early:** compressed blocks, the **Angel Block**, drums, the Golden Bag of Holding, the **Survival Generator**, the
  **Machine Block** and the Furnace Generator.
- **Mid:** the Glass Cutter makes Ender Shards, then the Redstone Crystal and **Resonator**. Also the **Enchanter**,
  the fuel-specific generators, Solar and Lunar Panels, and the Chicken Wing Ring.
- **End:** the **Ender Quarry** (needs a Netherite Pickaxe) with upgrades, the Netherstar Generator, Evil Infused
  Iron (Nether Star), the **Rainbow Generator** (needs one of every generator), Bedrockium and the Kikoku.

### Mowzie's Mobs

*1.8.2 · In-game guide: none (the advancements list the bosses)*

There's no crafting ladder, and bosses can be fought in any order. This split is a suggested difficulty order.

- **Early:** the mini-bosses and their drops. The **Naga** gives the Naga Fang Dagger, the Foliaath gives seeds, and
  the Grottol gives diamonds.
- **Mid:** the **Ferrous Wroughtnaut** drops the Axe of a Thousand Metals and the Wrought Helm. Sneak into the
  **Umvuthana Grove** wearing a mask and trade with **Umvuthi** for the **Sun's Blessing**.
- **End:** the **Frostmaw** drops the Ice Crystal. **Tongbi, the Sculptor**'s challenge gives the
  **Earthrend Gauntlet**, and he drops Geomancer armor.

### Alex's Mobs

*1.22.9 · In-game guide: Animal Dictionary (Book + Green Dye + a mob drop)*

- **Early:** the Animal Dictionary and simple mob-drop gear: Roadrunner Boots, the Antler Headdress, the Crocodile
  Chestplate, Cave Centipede Leggings.
- **Mid:** the Froststalker Helmet, Rock Shell Chestplate, Spiked Turtle Shell, Shield Of The Deep and Grappling
  Squok, and the Blood Sprayer and Hemolymph Blaster from Nether mosquitoes.
- **End:** the **Tarantula Hawk Elytra** (needs a vanilla Elytra). Summon the **Void Worm**: put a Crimson Mosquito
  Larva in a Capsid to get a Mysterious Worm, then throw it into the End void. It drops parts for the
  **Dimensional Carver** (also needs netherite).

## Tier 2 — End game mods

### Draconic Evolution

*3.1.2 · In-game guide: none (use JEI)*

**Early game**
- Mine **Draconium Ore**: deepslate at Y −64 to −48, the Nether, and the End.
- Make the **Draconium Core**, the Generator, and the **Fusion Crafting Core** with Draconium injectors.
- **Move on when:** you have a **Nether Star** from the **Wither**.

**Mid game**
- Make the **Wyvern Core** and **Wyvern Fusion Crafting Injectors**, then Wyvern tools and armor.
- Build the **Energy Core** multiblock with stabilizers and Energy Pylons.
- **Move on when:** you have a **Dragon Heart**. It floats above the exit portal after each Ender Dragon kill.

**End game**
- Fusion-craft **Awakened Draconium** with the Dragon Heart, then make the **Draconic Core** and Draconic gear.
- Find a **Chaos Island** (End, 10,000-block spacing). Defeat the **Chaos Guardian** for **Chaos Shards**.
- Chaos Shards and a **Dragon Egg** unlock the **Chaotic** tier.
- Build the **Draconic Reactor**.

**Needs from outside:** the Wither, the Ender Dragon (respawn it for more hearts), huge amounts of power.

### ProjectE

*PE1.0.1 · In-game guide: none*

ProjectE can technically be started on day one, but it's placed in Tier 2 on purpose: EMC can skip most other mods'
progression. See [STARTING_MODS.md](STARTING_MODS.md).

**Early game**
- Craft the **Philosopher's Stone**: world transmutation and a portable crafting grid.
- Build the **Transmutation Table**. Burning items there teaches them and adds EMC.
- Make **Alchemical Coal**, Covalence Dust and the **Alchemical Chest**.
- **Move on when:** you have an **Energy Condenser** and **Energy Collector MK1**.

**Mid game**
- **Anti-Matter Relays**.
- Upgrade fuel: **Mobius Fuel**, then **Aeternalis Fuel**. **Klein Stars** store EMC.
- Make **Dark Matter**, then DM tools, armor and the furnace, plus Collector and Relay MK2.
- **Move on when:** you can make **Red Matter**.

**End game**
- Red Matter gear, **Collector** and **Relay MK3**, and **Energy Condenser MK2**. There's no MK3 condenser.
- **Transmutation Tablet**, Dark Matter Pedestal, Gem armor and **Klein Star Omega**.
- **AppliedE** links EMC to AE2.

**Needs from outside:** nothing. That's why it can bypass other mods.

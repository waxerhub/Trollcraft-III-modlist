# Trollcraft III — Which Mods to Start With

This is a recommendation for where to put your first sessions. It combines:

- **The pack's own data:** what each mod needs before it can start, and what it unlocks in other mods. See
  [MOD_TIERS.md](MOD_TIERS.md) and [CROSS_REFERENCE.md](CROSS_REFERENCE.md).
- **Community advice** from guides, wikis and forum threads (see [Sources](#sources)).

---

## The short answer

Start these five in your first few sessions:

| # | Mod | What to do first | Why start it early |
|---|-----|------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Tinkers' Construct** | Part Builder, Tinker Station, then a smeltery | You need tools before anything else, and Materials and You is in your inventory from the start. It only needs clay, sand, gravel and copper |
| 2 | **Mystical Agriculture** | Mine Inferium and Prosperity Ore, then plant **Inferium Seeds** | Crops take real time to scale, so planting early pays off for the rest of the run. The official wiki says mining Inferium doesn't scale and seeds are the long-term source |
| 3 | **Botania** | Petal Apothecary, Pure Daisy, then a row of **Endoflames** burning charcoal | Mana builds up slowly. Endoflames are the standard early mana source, and players find early Botania slow enough that an addon exists just to speed it up |
| 4 | **Mekanism** | Heat Generator → Metallurgic Infuser → **Enrichment Chamber** | Your first power, steel and ore multiplication, from Overworld materials only. Its steel is also needed by Modular Powersuits and Extreme Reactors' Reinforced tier |
| 5 | **Create** | Water Wheel → Mechanical Press → Mixer (andesite age) | Cheap to start (andesite and iron), and it runs the same machines all game. The andesite stage needs no Nether trip |

Also, **scout for a meteorite** early. A Charger and a compass make a Meteorite Compass, and getting the presses and
budding certus home early makes the AE2 build easier later. You don't need to build the network yet.

---

## Why these five

1. **They need nothing from other dimensions or bosses.** Each one starts with Overworld materials. By contrast,
   Immersive Engineering's steel needs Nether Brick and Magma Blocks, Create's brass needs a blaze, and Draconic
   Evolution needs the Wither.
2. **They pay off over time.** Crops, mana and tool upgrades all build up. Starting them early means they're ready
   when you need them.
3. **They unlock other mods.** Early-mod items that later mods need:

   | Item from an early mod | Used by |
   |-----------------------|---------|
   | Mekanism steel | Modular Powersuits batteries, Extreme Reactors Reinforced tier |
   | Thermal electrum | Modular Powersuits' Ultimate Battery |
   | Thermal alloy blocks | Can stand in for Tinkers alloys when crafting the Tinker's Anvil |
   | Mekanism and Create ore processing | More ingots for every other mod |

4. **They teach the pack.** Older forum advice for new players was to learn **one tech mod at a time** rather than
   starting all of them. That's why only one main tech mod (Mekanism) is on the starter list, with Create as the
   build-focused second.

### What the community says, mod by mod

- **Mekanism:** early-game guides agree on the same opening: a Heat Generator (on lava for passive power), osmium,
  the Metallurgic Infuser for steel and Basic Control Circuits, then the Enrichment Chamber as the first step of ore
  processing. 3x processing comes later.
- **Create:** players in r/CreateMod suggest automating Andesite Alloy early, since a mixer gets more alloy per
  material, and building an assembly line for kinetic parts before moving on. Beginner packs advise mastering the
  Mechanical Press first and crafting Engineer's Goggles early to check stress.
- **Botania:** guides describe Endoflames as the early workhorse, usually a bank of 8–16 fed charcoal or coal
  blocks, before the Runic Altar and better flowers. An r/feedthebeast tips thread says the same.
- **Mystical Agriculture:** the official getting-started guide goes ores → seed bases → Inferium Seeds, and says
  essence farmland gives each tier 50% more Inferium.
- **AE2:** community wikis say to look for meteorites early even though AE2 is mostly a late-game storage mod.
  Reviewers describe it as fiddly early on and much better from the mid game. A common tip is to start with small
  (1k–4k) cells.
- **ProjectE:** the most consistent advice is to *wait*. Community wikis list its usual problems: infinite resources
  once a setup runs, skipping mining and farming, and early flight from Red Matter armor. Many packs gate it with
  quests, blacklists or balance addons. Trollcraft III has **no** ProjectE balance addon, so it's up to the player to
  hold off.

## Pick one main tech mod

You'll use most of them eventually, but going deep on one first is easier. Mekanism is the default. These are the
alternatives:

| Mod | Pick it first if you… | Cost to start | First thing that needs the Nether |
|-----|----------------------|---------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Mekanism** (default) | want power, steel and ore multiplication fast | Osmium, iron, redstone | Nothing until the late game |
| **Create** | like building visible machines | Andesite, iron or zinc | Brass (needs a blaze) |
| **Thermal** | want compact machines | Hand-crafted alloys with Fire Charges | Nothing essential |
| **Ender IO** | want conduits early | Grains of Infinity (a fire on bedrock) | Soularium, for the Soul Binder |
| **Immersive Engineering** | enjoy big multiblocks | Coke Oven bricks (clay, brick, sandstone) | Steel (Blast Bricks), so it's a slower start |

## Hold off on these

| Mod | Wait until | Why |
|-----|-----------|-----|
| **ProjectE** | Mid or late game | EMC can skip other mods' progression, and the pack doesn't rebalance it |
| **Draconic Evolution** | You can fight the Wither | Its Wyvern tier needs a Nether Star, and later tiers need the Ender Dragon and the Chaos Guardian |
| **RFTools Dimensions** | You have large, steady FE power | Dimensions drain power constantly and must stay chunkloaded |
| **Extreme Reactors 2** | Your generators can't keep up | Turbines need Cyanite from a running reactor, and the Reinforced tier needs steel |
| **Modular Powersuits** | You have steel (Mekanism or IE) | Even the Basic Battery needs steel, and the best one needs Thermal's electrum |
| **AE2 network** | You have the Inscriber and processors | Scout the meteorite early, but the network is a mid-game build |
| **Alex's Caves** | You have iron or better armor | The cave biomes are dangerous. Underground Cabins and Cave Tablets can be collected earlier |

## A suggested first few sessions

1. **Session 1:** Tinkers' Part Builder and Tinker Station tools. Mine Inferium and Prosperity Ore. Collect petals,
   build the Petal Apothecary and make a Pure Daisy. Look out for osmium, copper and zinc.
2. **Session 2:** Mekanism's Heat Generator, Metallurgic Infuser and Enrichment Chamber. Create's Water Wheel,
   Mechanical Press and Mixer. Plant Inferium Seeds and make an Infusion Crystal. Set up Endoflames, a Mana Spreader
   and a Mana Pool.
3. **Session 3:** the Tinkers' smeltery, a Meteorite Compass and a trip to the meteorite. Then get ready for the
   Nether.
4. **First Nether trip.** One trip can cover several mods:

   | Bring back | For |
   |------------|-----|
   | A blaze | Create's Blaze Burner (brass) |
   | Nether Brick and Magma Blocks | IE's Blast Bricks (steel) |
   | Nether Cobalt Ore, magma cream, soul sand | Tinkers' cobalt and Nether Grout |
   | Soul sand or soul soil | Ender IO's Soularium |
   | Soulstone with Soulium Ore | Mystical Agriculture mob seeds |
   | Nether quartz | AE2's Fluix Crystals |

From there, follow each mod's mid-game section in [MOD_TIERS.md](MOD_TIERS.md).
